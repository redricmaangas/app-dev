# My Favorite Movie

## The Dark Knight

One of my favorite movies is **The Dark Knight**. It's a superhero movie about Batman protecting Gotham City from the Joker.

### Why I Like It

- The story is interesting.
- The characters are well written.
- It has action and suspense.
- The Joker is one of my favorite villains.

### My Favorite Characters

1. Batman
2. Joker
3. Harvey Dent
